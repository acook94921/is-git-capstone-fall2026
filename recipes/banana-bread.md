# Recipe Name

**Contributed by: Auvion Cook**

## Ingredients

* &#x20;flour, sugar, baking soda, butter, eggs, and ripe bananas

## Steps

1. \*\*Preheat \& Prep:\*\* Preheat your oven to 350°F (175°C) and lightly grease a 4x8 inch loaf pan.
2. \*\*Mash \& Mix:\*\* In a large mixing bowl, mash the ripe bananas with a fork until smooth. Stir the melted butter directly into the mashed bananas.
3. \*\*Combine:\*\* Mix in the baking soda and salt. Stir in the sugar, beaten egg, and vanilla extract. Whisk thoroughly.
4. \*\*Add Flour:\*\* Gently fold in the all-purpose flour until the batter is just combined. Do not overmix.
5. \*\*Bake:\*\* Pour the batter into your prepared loaf pan. Bake for 50 to 60 minutes, or until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean.
6. \*\*Cool:\*\* Remove from the oven and let cool in the pan for 10 minutes before transferring to a wire rack.
